A `pattern` entry records something recurring, described in the person's own words — "I just
stop replying" rather than "exhibits conflict avoidance". `confidence: stated` when they said
it themselves; `confidence: inferred` when an agent noticed it and they agreed, and it stays
`inferred` until they state it in their own words.

Patterns are where interpretation is allowed to live. It is not allowed to leak into `person` or
`event` entries as adjectives.
