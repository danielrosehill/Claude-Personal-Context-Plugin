A `preference` entry says how the person wants to be engaged with: tone, directness, pacing,
vocabulary to avoid, what to do when they go quiet. These are almost always `scopes: [general]`
and `sensitivity: low` — they should reach every workspace.

This is the cheapest entry type to write and the one that most changes how a session goes.
Examples of real ones: "direct over reassuring", "don't summarise back what I just said", "don't
offer three options", "if I stop replying, wait — don't ask if I'm okay".
